## 🧮 CVM-4 Deterministic Econometric Equation

Unlike opaque "black-box" appraisal tools ([GoDaddy GoValue vs. DomainVoodoo Comparison](https://prstuvw.xyz/compare/godaddy-govalue-vs-domainvoodoo)), the **[DomainVoodoo CVM-4 Engine](https://prstuvw.xyz/how-it-works)** calculates three distinct liquidity tiers using a 6-factor econometric model:

$$\text{FMV}(d) = \mathcal{T}\left(\text{Base}_{\text{SLD}} \times M_{\text{TLD}} \times M_{\text{Length}} \times M_{\text{Commercial}} \times M_{\text{Phonetic}} \times M_{\text{Age}} - P_{\text{Risk}}, \text{Comps}_{k\text{-NN}}\right)$$

- **Wholesale / Investor Liquidation Floor (`~15%–28%` of Retail)**: Immediate 48-hour cash value on NamePros, GoDaddy Auctions, or NameJet.
- **Fair Market Value (`100%` Baseline)**: Expected 6–18 month clearing price with a funded Series A/B startup.
- **Strategic M&A / End-User Ceiling (`220%–350%+` of FMV)**: Defensive acquisition price for a Fortune 500 or AI unicorn buyer.
